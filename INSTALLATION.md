## Prerequisites

Before you begin, ensure you have:
- ✅ An iOS device (iPhone or iPad) running iOS 9.0 or later

- ✅ The `YamDisplay_Cracked.ipa` file downloaded

## Method 1: AltStore (Recommended)

AltStore is the easiest method for non-jailbroken devices and doesn't require a paid Apple Developer account.

### Step 1: Install AltStore on Your Computer

**For Windows:**
1. Download [AltServer for Windows](https://altstore.io/)
2. Install iTunes and iCloud from Apple (not Microsoft Store versions)
3. Run AltServer installer
4. Launch AltServer from system tray (near clock)

**For macOS:**
1. Download [AltServer for Mac](https://altstore.io/)
2. Open the `.dmg` file and drag AltServer to Applications
3. Launch AltServer (it appears in menu bar)
4. Grant any necessary permissions

### Step 2: Install AltStore on Your iOS Device

1. Connect your iOS device to your computer via USB
2. Trust the computer on your device when prompted
3. Click the AltServer icon in system tray/menu bar
4. Select **Install AltStore** → Choose your device
5. Enter your Apple ID credentials (stored locally only)
6. Wait for AltStore to install on your device

### Step 3: Trust the Developer Certificate

1. On your iOS device, go to **Settings** → **General** → **VPN & Device Management**
2. Find your Apple ID under "Developer App"
3. Tap your Apple ID and then tap **Trust**

### Step 4: Install Yam Display IPA

1. Transfer `YamDisplay_Cracked.ipa` to your iOS device:
   - Use AirDrop, email, cloud storage, or any file transfer method
   - Save it to Files app or compatible location

2. Open **AltStore** on your iOS device
3. Tap the **My Apps** tab
4. Tap the **+** button in the top-left corner
5. Browse and select `YamDisplay_Cracked.ipa`
6. Wait for installation to complete (may take 1-2 minutes)

### Step 5: Refresh Apps Weekly

⚠️ **Important**: Apps installed via AltStore expire after 7 days.

- Keep AltServer running on your computer
- Connect your device to the same WiFi network
- AltStore will auto-refresh apps when connected
- Or manually refresh by opening AltStore and tapping "Refresh All"

## Method 2: Sideloadly

Sideloadly is a popular alternative that works on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

### Step 1: Download and Install Sideloadly

1. Visit [sideloadly.io](https://sideloadly.io/)
2. Download the version for your operating system
3. Install and launch Sideloadly

### Step 2: Connect Your Device

1. Connect your iOS device to computer via USB
2. Trust the computer on your device when prompted
3. Sideloadly should detect your device automatically

### Step 3: Install the IPA

1. Drag and drop `YamDisplay_Cracked.ipa` into Sideloadly
   - Or click the IPA icon and browse to the file
2. Enter your Apple ID email address
3. Click **Start** button
4. Enter your Apple ID password when prompted
5. If you have 2FA enabled, enter the verification code
6. Wait for the installation to complete

### Step 4: Trust the Certificate

1. On your iOS device: **Settings** → **General** → **VPN & Device Management**
2. Tap your Apple ID under "Developer App"
3. Tap **Trust [Your Apple ID]**
4. Confirm by tapping **Trust** again

## Method 3: Jailbroken Devices

If your device is jailbroken, installation is much simpler.

### Requirements:
- Jailbroken iOS device
- AppSync Unified installed from Cydia/Sileo
- Filza File Manager (or similar)

### Installation Steps:

1. **Download the IPA** to your device:
   ```
   - Use Safari to download YamDisplay_Cracked.ipa
   - Or transfer via SSH/USB
   ```

2. **Open Filza File Manager**:
   - Navigate to where you downloaded the IPA
   - Usually in `/var/mobile/Documents/` or Downloads

3. **Install the IPA**:
   - Tap on `YamDisplay_Cracked.ipa`
   - Tap **Installer** (or **Install**)
   - Wait for installation to complete
   - Tap **Done**

4. **Launch the app**:
   - No need to trust certificates on jailbroken devices
   - App icon should appear on home screen immediately

## Post-Installation Setup

### First Launch:

1. **Launch Yam Display** from your home screen
2. Grant any requested permissions:
   - Local Network access
   - Notifications (optional)

3. **Configure Settings**:
   - Open the app
   - Go to Settings (if available)
   - Adjust display quality, frame rate, etc.

### Connect to Computer:

1. **Install Yam Display Server** on your computer:
   - Visit [yamstu.com](http://yamstu.com) or search for server software
   - Download and install the desktop component

2. **Ensure Same Network**:
   - Both device and computer must be on same WiFi
   - Check firewall settings allow connections

3. **Connect**:
   - Open Yam Display on iPad
   - It should detect your computer automatically
   - Tap to connect
   - Your iPad is now a second display!

## Troubleshooting

### Installation Failed

**"Unable to Install"**
- Delete any existing version of Yam Display
- Restart your device
- Try installation again
- Ensure iOS version is 9.0+

**"Untrusted Developer"**
- Go to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management
- Find and trust the developer certificate
- Try opening the app again

### App Crashes on Launch

1. **Force close and relaunch**:
   - Swipe up (or double-click home) to show app switcher
   - Swipe up on Yam Display to close
   - Relaunch the app

2. **Reinstall the app**:
   - Delete the app completely
   - Restart your device
   - Install again using your preferred method

3. **Check iOS compatibility**:
   - Yam Display requires iOS 9.0+
   - Some features may not work on older versions

### Certificate Expired

**AltStore Users**:
- Ensure AltServer is running on your computer
- Connect device to same WiFi
- Open AltStore and tap "Refresh All"

**Sideloadly Users**:
- Reconnect device to computer
- Reinstall the IPA using Sideloadly
- Apps expire every 7 days with free Apple ID

### Connection Issues

**Can't Find Computer**:
- Verify both devices on same WiFi network
- Restart both device and computer
- Check firewall isn't blocking connections
- Try disabling VPN temporarily

**Connection Drops**:
- Move closer to WiFi router
- Ensure stable WiFi connection
- Close other bandwidth-heavy apps
- Update router firmware if possible

### Performance Issues

**Lag or Stuttering**:
- Lower the display quality in settings
- Reduce frame rate
- Close background apps on iPad
- Ensure strong WiFi signal

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do I need to pay for an Apple Developer account?**  
A: No, a free Apple ID is sufficient for all non-jailbroken methods.

**Q: How often do I need to reinstall?**  
A: Free Apple IDs require re-signing every 7 days. Jailbroken devices have no expiration.

**Q: Is this safe to install?**  
A: The IPA is a decrypted version for educational purposes. Install at your own risk.

**Q: Can I use this without a computer?**  
A: After initial installation, yes. But you'll need a computer for weekly re-signing (non-jailbroken).

**Q: Does this work on the latest iOS version?**  
A: The app was built for iOS 10.2. It may work on newer versions but compatibility isn't guaranteed.
